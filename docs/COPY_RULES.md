# Copy rules for the screen passes

The client's words: "not too much writing. get rid of all the fluff." These rules bind every screen pass. DESIGN.md has the visual world; this file has the words.

1. No explanatory paragraphs on a screen. If a paragraph explains how the app works, delete it or move it behind a "?" (a `<details>` or a help link). Settings and Program are the worst offenders.
2. One short line per section at most, and only when the section's content cannot say it. A section title plus its rows needs no lede.
3. Labels are one or two words: "Forecast finish", "Slip", "Holding", "Waiting on", "Confirmed", "Stage". Sentence case. Never all caps, never tracked.
4. Never restate what the UI shows. "Why it moved" under a slip figure that is already a link is gone; the link's title carries it. "Tap a row to open the job" is gone; the row opens.
5. Headers carry no sentences. A page meta line is a fact ("Week of Mon 14 Sep. Compared with Monday's forecast.") or nothing.
6. Empty states are one line, an invitation to act: "No jobs on this side yet." "No notes yet. One line a day is plenty." Keep the asserted ones below verbatim.
7. Buttons say the action in two or three words: "Add photos", "Save new ETA", "Mark booked", "Fire reminders". Never "Submit", never "Click here".
8. Errors say the problem and the fix in one line: "Give it a name". "Needs signal".
9. Help text moves to a "?" control or is deleted. Prototype notes ("no push server in this prototype") stay only where a spec asserts them; otherwise they go.
10. Rule-bearing words are protected and keep their exact form: late ("14 days late"), unconfirmed ("Unconfirmed 9 days"), needed-by ("Needed Mon 2 Nov"), act-by ("Act by Mon 10 Aug"), expected, on plan, hold point, "none yet", "waiting to send", "Needs signal", "No signal".
